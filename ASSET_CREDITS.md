# Third-party assets / 第三方素材署名

## Mallard duck

“Mallard duck” by **Poly by Google**, via [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/frSLi6b6Vid), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Changes for this project: normalized the model to 0.50 m long, moved the origin to the belly waterline, and resized its base-color texture from 2048×2048 to 1024×1024. Instances use small scale differences and procedural swimming/bobbing in the lake. The original shape and coloration are retained. The author does not endorse this project.
