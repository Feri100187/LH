Downloaded original GLB: https://static.poly.pizza/b5b79a83-4dc0-4d6d-94ae-fb618129e1f7.glb

Changes made for this project: normalized the duck to 0.50 m length; placed the origin at the belly waterline; kept Blender -Y / glTF +Z as the forward direction; resized the original base-color texture from 2048×2048 to 1024×1024; renamed the mesh/material for game import. Original anatomy, topology and coloration are retained. The preview water surface, lights and cameras are not part of the exported duck.
